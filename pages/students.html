Wellbeing

Support around the school day

Every student is known by a class teacher, a subject teacher network and the guidance office.

  • Class teacher for every grade, meeting daily before the first period
  • Guidance and counselling office, open weekdays 8 a.m. – 3 p.m.
  • Library and laboratory access outside teaching hours by roster
  • Scholarship and mid-day meal support administered through the class teacher
  • Boarding facilities with residential wardens for out-station students
  • Prefect, student council and service clubs open by election from Grade 7
